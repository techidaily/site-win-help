## Aggregate Your Audio Sources with MAC: Mastering the Group Function in Music Production

[![Creating an aggregate audio device on MAC](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/createaggregateaudiodevice-coverimage2.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/createaggregateaudiodevice-coverimage2.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "creating an aggregate audio device MAC")

**Pro Tip For MAC DJ Software Users | This post explains the process of creating an ‘Aggregate Device’ with the [Audio MIDI Setup](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/List%5Fof%5FOS%5FX%5Fcomponents#Archive%5FUtility) utility in Mac OS X computers.**

DJs using a MAC with one of our [professional DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) applications and NOT using a [pro audio interface](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or a **DJ controller** with one baked in (with ins/outs/mic support) may wish to create an **aggregate audio device** so they can utilize both the output (main output) and mic (input) simultaneously.

On most MAC’s the **audio output device** (typically labeled ‘Built-in Output’) and the **audio input device** – the **microphone** (usually called ‘Built-in Microphone’) are considered separate devices, so you cannot use them at the same time in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), [DEX 3 LE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [LYRX](http://lyrxkaraoke.com/). You have the option to make an **aggregate device** from the 2 devices (or more) to be able to **simultaneously utilize both output and input** (microphone).

**If you wish to aggregate two or more audio devices (combined them into one) so they would appear in our DJ software or LYRX as one single audio device here’s what you do:**

[Step 1 | Open a Finder window and go to Applications -> Utilities and start ‘Audio MIDI Setup’ ![Creating an aggregate device MAC step 1](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/700px-Aggregate_step1.jpg?fit=700%2C388&ssl=1 "Creating an aggregate device MAC step 1")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/700px-Aggregate%5Fstep1.jpg?fit=700%2C388&ssl=1)

[Step 2 | If the Audio Devices window is not already opened you need to go to the Menu -> Window -> ‘Show Audio Devices’ ![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/500px-Aggregate_step2.jpg?fit=500%2C174&ssl=1 "Creating an aggregate device on MAC step 2")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/500px-Aggregate%5Fstep2.jpg?fit=500%2C174&ssl=1)

[Step 3 | On the bottom-left corner press the + (plus) button and then select ‘Create Aggregate Device’ ![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/700px-Aggregate_step3.jpg?fit=700%2C549&ssl=1 "Creating an aggregate device MAC step 3")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/700px-Aggregate%5Fstep3.jpg?fit=700%2C549&ssl=1)

[Step 4 | A new ‘Aggregate Device’ will appear in the device list and ‘Built-in Output’ devices. After you checked (enabled) both the input and the output device, then select the ‘Clock Source’ as the ‘Built-in Output’ device ![Creating an aggregate device mac step 4](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/700px-Aggregate_step4_5.jpg?fit=700%2C514&ssl=1 "Creating an aggregate device mac step 4")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/700px-Aggregate%5Fstep4%5F5.jpg?fit=700%2C514&ssl=1)

That concludes the setup in audio midi. Now launch our **DJ software** or [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com) and select ‘**Aggregate Device**‘ under the ‘**Select Output Device**‘ drop down menu and configure the mic input.
