_Available for: Windows/Mac_

Creating the perfect mix often requires the isolation of vocals from a track, and the Ultimate Vocal Remover stands out as a premier tool to do just that. With its capability to extract high-quality acapellas from any song, it serves as a priceless utility for DJs. Its interface might seem a bit technical at first glance, but once you get the hang of it, you’ll find it just as effective, if not more, as its paid counterparts.

**Download:** [Ultimate Vocal Remover](https://ultimatevocalremover.com/)

_Available for: Windows/Mac_

**PCDJ DEX 3 LE** is a free edition of PCDJ’s renowned professional DJ software, DEX 3\. This Lite Edition offers an intuitive interface, seamless DJ controller support, and a rich feature set that allows DJs to mix, scratch, and produce beats with ease. While it might be the free version, it doesn’t skimp on quality or capability. Its functionalities make it a formidable tool for both newbie DJs trying to find their footing and seasoned pros looking for reliable software.

**Download:** [PCDJ DEX 3 LE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

### 3\. VB-Cable (Windows) & BlackHole (Mac)
_Available for: Windows (VB-Cable) / Mac (BlackHole)_

Capturing the essence of a track or grabbing that elusive sample from a live session or any other source becomes a breeze with VB-Cable and BlackHole. These utilities allow DJs to record any audio playing on the computer seamlessly. Their straightforward setup and functionality make them a must-have for DJs looking to incorporate unique sounds and samples into their mixes.

**Download:** [VB-Cable for Windows](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) | [BlackHole for Mac](https://existential.audio/blackhole/)

### 4\. Audacity
_Available for: Windows/Mac/Linux_

**Audacity** stands out as a beacon in the world of audio editing. Its extensive range of features, from simple cut, copy, paste, and delete to more complex operations like multi-track editing, audio spectrum analysis, and effects application, makes it indispensable. Whether you’re looking to optimize recordings, remove noise, or apply filters, Audacity has got you covered.

**Download:** [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/)

### 5\. Hydrogen
_Available for: Windows/Mac/Linux_

Every DJ understands the significance of beats and rhythms. Hydrogen is an advanced drum machine software that’s free and open source. It lets DJs create natural sounding drum beats with ease. With its user-friendly interface, layering beats and customizing drum patterns becomes a piece of cake. This utility is perfect for DJs wanting to add a personal touch to their sets.

**Download:** [Hydrogen](http://hydrogen-music.org/)
